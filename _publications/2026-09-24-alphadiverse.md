<small>(Work done during an internship at Shanghai Non-convex Intelligent Technology.)</small>

**Qingzhuo Wang**, Zikun Wei, Zhihua Wei, Wen Shen

**AlphaDiverse** combines multi-agent alpha factor research, diverse research path collection, and post-training of local agents. Complementary research plans and varied research environments produce diverse traces for supervised fine-tuning of local **Planner** and **Realizer** agents. A **joint GRPO** method then optimizes both agents using rewards for predictive quality and complementary factor contributions.

Research feedback is restricted to an inner data period, with the frozen final model evaluated on a later outer period. Experiments across four Chinese stock universes show competitive predictive performance alongside broader exploration of economic mechanisms.
