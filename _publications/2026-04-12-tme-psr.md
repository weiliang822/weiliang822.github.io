**TME-PSR** integrates time-aware, multi-interest, and explanation personalization for sequential recommendation. A **dual-view gated time encoder** captures individual temporal rhythms for recommendation and explanation. A lightweight **multihead Linear Recurrent Unit (LRU)** architecture models fine-grained sub-interests efficiently.

A **dynamic dual-branch mutual information weighting mechanism** adapts the semantic alignment between recommendations and explanations to each user. Experiments on three real-world datasets show improved recommendation accuracy and explanation quality with lower computational cost than the evaluated baselines.
